01About this policy

This policy applies to Fyrlo (the "App"), a habit tracker for Android and iOS, published on Google Play and the Apple App Store under the package name dev.fishfactory.fyrlo.

The App is developed by Fish Factory ("Fish Factory", "we", "us", "our"), an independent mobile development studio registered in Ukraine. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for any privacy question or request: contact@fishfactory.dev.

This policy covers the App only. The Fish Factory website and our other apps are covered by the general Fish Factory Privacy Policy.

02At a glance

  • The App needs an account: you sign in with Apple or Google. We do not ask for a password, your name or a phone number.
  • Your habits and progress are stored in your account on our servers, so they stay safe and appear on every phone you sign in to.
  • The App has no ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and does not read your advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the App at any time.

03Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address as given by Apple or Google. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only the relay address Apple creates for the App.
  • Sign-in identifiers — the account identifier from Apple and/or Google and which of them are linked to your Fyrlo account.
  • Sign in with Apple token — kept on our server only so that we can revoke the App's access to your Apple ID when you delete your account.

Habits and progress you create

  • Habits — name, icon, type (Once, Count, Timer, Steps or Quit), goal, schedule, the names of a habit's steps, reminder times and check-in time.
  • Progress — what you log each day: taps and amounts, timer time, ticked steps, check-ins, slips and cravings resisted, with the time they were made.
  • Results in the App — streaks, success rates, Diamonds, freezes, achievements and your archive of past habits.
  • Notification Center — the in-app list of reminders and messages, and when they were read or cleared.
  • Settings — for example the theme and accent colour you choose.

Habit names are free text. Please keep in mind that what you type — for example, a Quit habit such as "Quit smoking" — may say something about your health or lifestyle. We use it only to show and sync your habits, and never for any other purpose.

Device and sync information

  • Time zone of the device you use, which becomes your account's time zone so your days end at your local midnight.
  • Device records — which of your devices are signed in, their platform, app version and when they last synced, so the server knows when every device has caught up before a day is judged.
  • Push token — a token from Firebase Cloud Messaging that lets our server send notifications to your devices.

Subscriptions

Fyrlo is offered as a subscription with a free trial. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive your card number or other payment details. Through RevenueCat, we receive what is needed to unlock the App: an anonymous app user ID linked to your account, the plan, trial and subscription status, renewal and expiry dates, store transaction identifiers, and the store country and currency.

Crash reports

When the App crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report: the stack trace, app version, device model, operating system version, and a random identifier for the App installation. We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs.

Messages to us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

What we do not collect

The App does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health and fitness data from other apps. It does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

04Device permissions

  • Notifications — to ring your habit reminders, the end-of-day reminder, and a heads-up before your free trial ends. The App asks after you create your first habit; the App works without it, but reminders will not ring.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android 14 and later, optional) — lets reminders ring exactly on time instead of a few minutes late.
  • Internet access — to sign in, sync your data and manage your subscription.

Habit reminders are scheduled on your device. Only the free-trial reminder and the subscription warning are sent from our server. You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings.

05How we use information

  • To create and run your account and sign you in with Apple or Google.
  • To store your habits and progress, apply the App's rules (streaks, Diamonds, achievements) on our server, and keep every device you use in sync.
  • To send reminders and subscription notifications.
  • To verify purchases and give access to the App while your trial or subscription is active.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To prevent abuse and to protect the security of the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting rules.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

08International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States and countries in the European Economic Area. Where data is transferred from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer relies on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

09Data retention

  • Account, habits and progress — for as long as your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Notification Center entries — shown in the App for 30 days; kept on our server for at most 1 year, then deleted.
  • Crash reports — up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records — for as long as tax, accounting and consumer-protection laws require.
  • Support correspondence — up to 24 months after the request is closed.

Backups that contain deleted data are overwritten on their normal rotation schedule, within 90 days.

10Deleting your account

In the App: open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This is also available on the Subscription screen if your subscription has ended. Deletion happens straight away and removes your account and every record of it — habits, progress, Diamonds and achievements — on every device. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the App's access to your Apple ID.

Without the App: follow the steps on our Data deletion page, or email contact@fishfactory.dev with the subject "Data deletion request — Fyrlo" from the email address of your account. We complete such requests within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Signing out does not delete anything: your data stays in your account until you delete it.

11Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data or delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that has already taken place;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@fishfactory.dev. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

California and other US states

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can exercise the rights to know, delete and correct by contacting us at the address above.

12Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between the App and our servers. In our database, access rules ensure that each account can reach only its own records, and every change to your data is checked on the server. Access by our team is limited to people who need it to run the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if we become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

13Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, up to 16), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App and the law change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will tell you in the App before it takes effect.
